DEFENSE FIRST

CYBER SECURITY LEARNING

01 / ACCOUNT SECURITY

Use strong unique passwords, passkeys or 2FA, recovery methods, login alerts, and review active sessions regularly.

02 / NETWORK BASICS

Understand HTTPS, DNS, Wi‑Fi security, VPNs, public networks, and why encryption matters.

03 / ETHICAL TESTING

Practice only on systems you own or have explicit permission to test. Learn threat modeling and defensive validation.

PHISHING AWARENESS

RED FLAGS TO SPOT

Urgent threats • unexpected login links • misspelled domains • requests for OTPs/passwords • suspicious attachments • “too good to be true” offers.

Rule: never share passwords or one-time codes. When unsure, open the service through its official app/site instead of clicking the message link.
